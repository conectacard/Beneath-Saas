What is BENEATH?

A cybersecurity tool for private communication without sharing your phone number, email address, or personal data. It is not an entertainment messaging service.

Transmit, don’t store (48 / 72 hours)

Messages and files are purged from infrastructure once delivered. Files expire after 48 to 72 hours. Download them before they are gone.

Ideal for:

  • Sports Clubs: Direct and secure messaging.
  • Schools: Communication between teachers and parents without exposing personal numbers.
  • Companies & Professional Services: Confidential exchange of documents and data with clients, suppliers, or candidates without exposing personal contact details.

Global Compliance & Design Standards

BENEATH has been designed taking as a reference international principles, frameworks, and best practices in cybersecurity and data protection.

Core Design Principles

  • Data Minimization: Designed to facilitate communication without requiring the unnecessary exchange of personal numbers or emails.
  • Limited Retention: Transmitted files and messages remain temporarily available and are automatically purged.
  • Privacy by Design: Data protection is embedded from the earliest stages of platform architecture.
  • Privacy by Default: Communications operate under a private, controlled model where access depends strictly on invitation and authorization.

Design References & Frameworks

Our operational model aligns with recognized international guidelines and references:

  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 & Privacy Framework
  • GDPR & EDPB Data Protection Principles (Minimization, Storage Limitation, Integrity & Confidentiality)
  • ISO/IEC Reference Controls for personally identifiable information (PII) protection.
